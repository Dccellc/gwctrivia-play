Green White Checkered: daily NASCAR trivia

Five questions a day about NASCAR drivers and their cars: who drove the car in the photo, what season it is from, what number it carried, which track a win came at, and where the driver is from. Close answers still score, up to 1,000 points a day, and a new set comes out every day at 4 a.m. Eastern.

Facts come from Wikipedia and Wikidata, or the gwctrivia.com team's independent research, and photos from Wikimedia Commons. Not affiliated with or endorsed by NASCAR, its teams, drivers or sponsors.
